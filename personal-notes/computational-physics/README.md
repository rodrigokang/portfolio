# Computational Physics
***Technical Notes***  
**Author:** Rodrigo Kang

Computational physics uses numerical and computational methods to investigate physical systems and phenomena that can be formulated and explored through computation.

These notes approach physical problems through a combination of mathematical modelling, numerical methods, and programming. The emphasis is on understanding the underlying physical principles, translating them into computational models, and using numerical experiments to study their behaviour.

The material may include theoretical developments, mathematical derivations, numerical methods, simulations, visualisations, and implementations. The aim is not to provide an exhaustive treatment of physics, but to develop a clear and computationally grounded understanding of physical problems as they arise in different contexts.
