# Quantitative Finance
***Technical Notes***  
**Author:** Rodrigo Kang

This directory contains systematic notes on quantitative finance, with an emphasis on the mathematical, statistical, and computational methods used to model financial markets and solve quantitative problems.

The notes develop the underlying theory alongside its computational implementation, connecting mathematical models with their interpretation, assumptions, numerical treatment, and practical use.

## Scope

The material may cover topics such as asset pricing, portfolio theory, financial econometrics, derivatives, stochastic models, risk modelling, and computational methods in finance.

Particular attention is given to the mathematical structures underlying quantitative models, including probability and stochastic processes, optimisation, statistical inference, differential equations, and numerical methods.

## Approach

The notes are developed from first principles where this helps clarify the assumptions and structure of a model. Analytical derivations are complemented by computational methods when closed-form solutions are unavailable, impractical, or insufficient for studying the behaviour of the system.

The emphasis is not only on applying established formulas or algorithms, but also on understanding:

* the assumptions behind a model;
* how the model is derived;
* what its parameters and outputs represent;
* how it can be estimated, simulated, or solved computationally;
* where its limitations arise; and
* how theoretical results relate to financial problems.
