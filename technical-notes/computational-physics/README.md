# Computational Physics

***Technical Notes***  
**Author:** Rodrigo Kang

This directory contains technical notes accompanying selected projects in Computational Physics.

The notes provide detailed treatments of the physical principles, mathematical formulations, and computational methods underlying these projects, with particular attention to theoretical developments and analytical derivations that warrant further explanation.

## Scope

The material addresses mathematical and computational aspects of physical problems, including analytical methods, numerical techniques, and the modelling and simulation of physical systems.

Topics are developed in connection with specific projects, with emphasis on the theoretical foundations and methodological details necessary to understand the approaches employed.

## Approach

The notes develop physical and mathematical principles from first principles where appropriate, connecting theoretical formulations with analytical and computational methods.

Particular attention is given to:

* the physical assumptions underlying mathematical models;
* the derivation and interpretation of governing equations;
* the analytical and numerical methods used to investigate physical systems;
* the relationship between theoretical formulations and computational implementation;
* the validity and limitations of mathematical and computational approximations; and
* the interpretation and verification of results.

The treatment is intended to complement the corresponding projects without duplicating their main discussion of methods, implementation, and results.
