# Computer Science
***Technical Notes***  
**Author:** Rodrigo Kang

Computer science provides the theoretical and computational foundations for representing information, designing algorithms, and developing systems that solve problems through computation.

These notes explore computational concepts from both theoretical and practical perspectives. The emphasis is on understanding the principles behind algorithms and programming, the reasoning involved in designing computational solutions, and the relationship between abstract ideas and their concrete implementation.

The material may include theoretical developments, algorithmic analysis, programming concepts, computational examples, and implementations. The aim is not to provide an exhaustive treatment of computer science, but to develop a clear and reusable understanding of the concepts and methods as they arise in different contexts.
