# Numerical Optimisation
***Technical Notes***  
**Author:** Rodrigo Kang

Numerical optimisation concerns the computational methods used to find solutions to optimisation problems when analytical solutions are unavailable, impractical, or computationally prohibitive.

These notes focus on the mathematical formulation, numerical behaviour, and implementation of optimisation methods. The emphasis is on understanding how the algorithms work, the assumptions under which they operate, and the practical considerations that arise when they are applied to real problems.

The material may include theoretical developments, algorithmic descriptions, numerical examples, and implementations. The aim is not to provide an exhaustive treatment of numerical optimisation, but to develop a clear and reusable understanding of the methods as they arise in different contexts.
