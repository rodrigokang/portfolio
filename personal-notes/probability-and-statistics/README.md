# Probability & Statistics
***Technical Notes***  
**Author:** Rodrigo Kang

Probability and statistics provide a mathematical framework for reasoning about uncertainty, variability, and information derived from data.

These notes cover probabilistic and statistical concepts from both theoretical and applied perspectives. The emphasis is on understanding the assumptions behind probabilistic models, the principles of statistical inference, and the relationship between mathematical formulations and observed data.

The material may include theoretical developments, derivations, computational methods, numerical examples, and implementations. The aim is not to provide an exhaustive treatment of probability and statistics, but to develop a clear and reusable understanding of the concepts and methods as they arise in different contexts.
