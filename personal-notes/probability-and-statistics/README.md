# Probability & Statistics
**Technical Notes**
**Author:** Rodrigo Kang

This directory contains systematic notes on probability and statistics, with an emphasis on the mathematical foundations of uncertainty, random phenomena, statistical inference, and data analysis.

The notes develop probabilistic and statistical concepts from their theoretical foundations through to their analytical and computational treatment, connecting mathematical models of uncertainty with methods for learning from data.

## Scope

The material may cover topics such as probability theory, random variables and distributions, multivariate probability, stochastic processes, limit theorems, statistical inference, estimation, hypothesis testing, regression, Bayesian methods, and computational statistics.

Particular attention is given to the mathematical structure of probabilistic models, the assumptions underlying statistical procedures, the properties of estimators and inferential methods, and the interpretation of statistical results.

## Approach

The notes are developed from first principles where this helps clarify the mathematical reasoning behind probabilistic and statistical results. Formal derivations are complemented by computational methods, simulations, and numerical experiments where appropriate.

The emphasis is not only on applying statistical methods, but also on understanding:

* how uncertainty is represented mathematically;
* how probabilistic models are constructed and interpreted;
* the assumptions underlying statistical models and procedures;
* how estimators and inferential methods are derived;
* the theoretical properties of statistical procedures;
* how uncertainty is quantified and communicated;
* how computational methods support probabilistic and statistical analysis; and
* where particular models or inferential assumptions cease to be appropriate.
